## Full description (1560/4000 characters)

```
🇬🇭 QareGO brings rides, deliveries and home services together in one app for Ghana.

🏍️ BOOK A RIDE
• Choose a motorbike, Pragya or car
• See the fare before you book
• Track your rider live on the map, and share your code only when they arrive

🍲 FOOD, GROCERIES AND PHARMACY
• Order from restaurants, chop bars, shops and pharmacies near you
• Add a prescription photo when your medicine needs one
• Follow your order from the store to your door

🔥 COOKING GAS
• Refill: a rider collects your empty cylinder, fills it at the nearest station and brings it back
• Exchange: a rider brings a full cylinder and takes your empty one
• Choose your cylinder size and see the total before you order

👕 LAUNDRY
• A rider collects your clothes and takes them to a laundry shop near you
• Book a pickup time that suits you
• The shop counts your items. If the price comes out higher than your estimate, nothing is washed until you agree
• Your clean clothes come back to your door

📦 PARCELS
• Send a parcel across town, or have a rider collect one from a shop or sender for you
• Watch it on the map until it's delivered

💳 PAY YOUR WAY
• Pay with cash, mobile money or card

💰 RIDE AND EARN
Riders use the same app. Go on duty when it suits you, see what you'll earn on a trip before you accept it, and keep track of your earnings.

🏪 FOR BUSINESSES
Restaurants, shops, pharmacies, gas stations and laundries can sell on QareGO and manage orders from a phone, tablet or laptop. Register on WhatsApp: 050 177 4210.
```
