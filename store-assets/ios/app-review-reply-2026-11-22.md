Thank you for the review. We have fixed both issues. Please review version 1.0.6 (BUILD).

Guideline 5.1.2(i) - Data Use and Sharing

QareGO does not track users. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, we do not link the data we collect with third-party data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers. We use the name, phone number, email address, address and location only to provide the rides, deliveries and orders the customer asks for, and for customer support. Our App Privacy answers wrongly marked this data as "used to track you". We have corrected them in App Store Connect: no data type is now marked as used for tracking, so App Tracking Transparency is not needed.

Guideline 5.1.1(v) - Data Collection and Storage

The app no longer asks people to register before they can look around. On the first screen, tap "Browse without an account". Without an account you can:
- see all services (rides, parcels, food, groceries, pharmacy, cooking gas, laundry)
- open any restaurant, shop, pharmacy or laundry and view its full menu or price list with prices
- see delivery fees, ride and parcel fares, gas prices and parcel pickup points

We only ask the customer to sign in for account-based features: placing an order, booking a ride or parcel, sending a prescription, booking a laundry pickup, and viewing their orders, notifications and profile. When they tap one of these, the app explains why and offers "Sign in"; after signing in they return to the same screen to finish.

To test the signed-in features, the demo customer login from our earlier notes still works: phone number 22222222.
