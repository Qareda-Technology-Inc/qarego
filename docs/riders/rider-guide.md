## 1. Joining QareGO

1. **Download the QareGO app** from Google Play or the App Store. It's the same app customers use; choose **Rider** when it asks.
2. **Sign in** with your phone number and the one-time code we send by SMS.
3. **Complete your profile:**
   - your full name and a clear profile photo;
   - vehicle type: **motorbike**, **pragya** (tricycle) or **car**;
   - your number plate;
   - photos of your **driver's licence** (front and back) and your **Ghana Card**.
4. **Wait for approval.** QareGO checks your documents. If anything is missing or unclear, we tell you what to fix. When you're approved, the app lets you go on duty.

You must be at least 18, hold a valid licence for your vehicle, and keep your vehicle roadworthy and insured as the law requires.

## 2. Going on duty and getting offers

- Tap **Go on duty** to receive offers, and **Go off duty** when you stop. Keep the app open and location on while on duty, so customers can follow you and QareGO can find jobs near you.
- **Choose your services** in the app: rides, parcels and store deliveries. You only get offers for what you've switched on and what your vehicle can do.
- Each **offer** rings and shows the pickup, the drop-off and **what you'll earn**. Accept it or let it go.

**Declining offers.** You're free to decline, but each decline counts as a **reliability strike** for that service. At **5 strikes**, offers for that service pause for **24 hours**. Every job you complete removes a strike. (These numbers are QareGO's current settings and may change; the app shows your reliability.)

## 3. Doing a job

### Rides
1. Go to the pickup point. The app navigates you there.
2. When you meet the passenger, ask for the **4-digit code** on their screen and enter it. This confirms you have the right person.
3. Start the trip and take them to the destination.
4. End the trip. The app shows **what to collect**:
   - **Cash:** collect the fare shown.
   - **Mobile money:** the customer pays in the app after the trip. You'll see "Waiting for the customer to pay". You don't need to wait for it.

### Parcels
1. Go to the pickup point and collect the parcel.
2. Take it to the drop-off.
3. Ask the person receiving it for the **4-digit delivery code** and enter it to complete the delivery.
4. Collect cash if the app says so. Mobile money works as for rides.

### Store deliveries (food, groceries, pharmacy)
1. Go to the store. The app shows when the order is ready.
2. Collect the order and check it's the right one.
3. **Cash orders:** pay the store the amount the app shows when you collect (the store's share). You collect the full order total from the customer.
4. Take it to the customer and enter their **4-digit delivery code** to complete it.
5. **Cash:** collect the total shown. **Mobile money:** already paid; nothing to collect.

**Never skip a code step**: the code is how the app knows the right person got the ride or order. Ask for it only face to face, never by phone or message.

## 4. Special deliveries

- **Gas refill:** collect the customer's empty cylinder, take it to the gas station, then take the full one back. **Gas exchange:** take a full cylinder from the station to the customer and bring their empty one back to the station.
- **Laundry:** collect the customer's bag and take it to the laundry. Later a rider (maybe you, maybe another rider) takes it back. When two riders share a laundry job, the delivery pay is **split in half** between them automatically.
- **Pharmacy:** you see how many items there are, not the names of the medicines. Keep the order sealed.
- **Parcels:** don't carry anything illegal or dangerous. You may refuse a parcel you reasonably believe breaks the rules.

## 5. Your money

### What you earn

| Job | What you keep |
|---|---|
| **Ride or parcel** | The fare minus QareGO's commission. Default commission: **15%**. |
| **Store delivery** | The **whole delivery fee**. |

The offer shows your earnings before you accept, and the **Earnings** screen shows the gross fare, the commission and your net for every job.

*Example:* a GH₵40 ride at 15% commission → you keep **GH₵34**.

### How the money reaches you

| How the customer pays | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | You keep the cash. QareGO's commission (and, for store orders, QareGO's share of the order) is **charged to your wallet**. |
| **Mobile money, store delivery** | Your delivery pay is **sent straight to your MoMo** when you deliver. It shows in your history as "Sent to your MoMo". If the transfer fails, it's added to your QareGO wallet instead. |
| **Mobile money, ride or parcel** | When the customer pays, the fare minus commission is **added to your QareGO wallet**. |

### Your wallet

- **Positive balance** = money QareGO holds for you. **Cash out** to your MoMo any time from **Earnings** (minimum **GH₵1**). It also covers the commission on your next cash jobs.
- **Negative balance** = commission you owe from cash jobs. Pay it with **Top up** in **Earnings** (mobile money), or in cash to QareGO.
- **Debt limit:** if you owe more than **GH₵100**, you can't go on duty until you top up. Once you owe nothing, you're active again straight away.

### If a mobile money customer doesn't pay

Sometimes a customer chooses mobile money but pays you in cash instead. On the trip screen, or later in your trip history, tap **Customer paid me cash**. The trip then counts as a cash trip: you keep the cash, and the commission is charged to your wallet. Only tap it if you really received the cash.

A customer who hasn't paid for a mobile money trip can't book again until they pay, and QareGO follows up unpaid trips.

## 6. Cancellations

- **Customers** can cancel a ride or parcel while it's being arranged or during the trip.
- **If a store cancels** an order after you've set off, you're paid: **half** the delivery fee once you've accepted, the **full** fee once you've reached the store or are carrying the order. It's added to your wallet.
- **If you can't complete a job**, contact the customer and QareGO support straight away. Don't just abandon it.

## 7. Rules and good practice

- Follow traffic laws. Wear a helmet on a motorbike, and give your passenger one.
- Be polite and patient. Customers rate every trip.
- Keep orders safe, upright and sealed. Don't open or tamper with anything you carry.
- Use a customer's phone number only to complete their job. Don't contact them afterwards.
- Never share your account. One rider, one account.
- Pay what you owe. Riders who don't settle commission can be suspended.

Serious or repeated problems (unsafe riding, fraud, abuse, false "paid in cash" claims, tampering with orders) can lead to suspension or removal.
