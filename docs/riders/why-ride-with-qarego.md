# Why ride with QareGO

**Earn with your motorbike, pragya or car, on your own time.** QareGO sends you ride, parcel and delivery jobs near you. You choose which ones to take.

## What you get

**Work when you want**
- Go **on duty** when you want to work and **off duty** when you don't. No shifts, no minimum hours.
- Choose the kind of jobs you want: **rides**, **parcels**, and **deliveries** from restaurants, shops, pharmacies, gas stations and laundries.

**See your pay before you accept**
- Every job offer shows **what you'll earn** and where you're going before you accept.
- Jobs are offered to riders **close by**, so you spend less time riding empty.

**Keep most of what you earn**
- **Rides and parcels:** you keep the fare minus QareGO's commission (default **15%**).
- **Store deliveries:** you keep the **whole delivery fee**.
- **Cash jobs:** the customer pays you on the spot.
- **Mobile money jobs:** store delivery pay goes straight to your MoMo. Ride and parcel fares go into your QareGO wallet, and you **cash out to MoMo any time**.

**Paid even when plans change**
- If a store cancels after you've set off, you're still paid for the trip: **half** the delivery fee once you've accepted, the **full** fee once you've reached the store or are carrying the order.

**Safe and simple**
- Passengers and receivers confirm with a **4-digit code**, so you always have the right person and the right order.
- Built-in **map and navigation**, **chat** and **calling** with the customer, with their number shown only to the rider on the job.
- Your earnings, commission and wallet are all in the **Earnings** screen.

## What you need

- A **motorbike, pragya (tricycle) or car**, roadworthy and insured as the law requires
- A valid **driver's licence** for your vehicle
- Your **Ghana Card**
- Your vehicle's **number plate**
- A smartphone with the **QareGO app** and mobile data

## How to join

1. Download the **QareGO** app (Google Play or the App Store) and choose **Rider**.
2. Sign in with your phone number and the code we text you.
3. Add your details: name, vehicle type, number plate, and photos of your licence (front and back) and Ghana Card.
4. QareGO checks your documents. When you're approved, go on duty and start earning.

The [rider guide](rider-guide.md) explains everything step by step.
