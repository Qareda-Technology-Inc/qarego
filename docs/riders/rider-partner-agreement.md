## 1. What this agreement is for

1.1 QareGO runs a platform (the QareGO app and related services) that connects customers who want rides, parcel deliveries and store deliveries with riders who provide them.

1.2 This agreement sets the terms on which you use QareGO to receive and complete jobs, and how you're paid.

## 2. You are independent

2.1 You are an independent service provider, not an employee, worker or agent of QareGO. You choose whether, when and where to go on duty, and which offers to accept.

2.2 You provide your own vehicle, phone, data, fuel, maintenance and equipment, at your own cost.

2.3 You are responsible for your own taxes, and for any registration your work requires.

## 3. Joining and staying approved

3.1 To be approved you must provide: your full name, a profile photo, your vehicle type (motorbike, pragya or car), its number plate, your driver's licence (front and back) and your Ghana Card. QareGO may ask for more, for example vehicle registration or insurance documents.

3.2 You confirm that you are at least 18; that you hold a valid licence for your vehicle; and that your vehicle is roadworthy, registered and insured as the law requires for the work you do.

3.3 You must tell QareGO straight away if your licence is suspended or expires, your vehicle changes, or your insurance lapses. Don't go on duty until it's sorted out.

3.4 One rider, one account. Don't let anyone else use your account or ride in your place.

## 4. Doing jobs

4.1 Each offer shows the pickup, the drop-off and what you will earn. You may accept or decline it.

4.2 Declining or ignoring offers counts towards a reliability limit set by QareGO (currently: 5 strikes pause offers for that service for 24 hours; each completed job removes one). The app shows your reliability.

4.3 When you accept a job, you agree to complete it promptly and safely, following the app's steps, including confirming the customer's 4-digit code. Never complete a job without the right code, and never ask a customer for their code except in person.

4.4 Handle every order and parcel with care. Keep it sealed, and don't open, tamper with or swap anything. For pharmacy orders, keep the contents private.

4.5 You may refuse a parcel you reasonably believe is illegal or dangerous. Tell QareGO support.

4.6 Follow traffic laws at all times. On a motorbike, wear a helmet and provide one for your passenger.

4.7 Treat customers, store staff and their property with respect. No harassment, discrimination, abuse or unsafe behaviour.

## 5. Customer information

5.1 You'll see some customer details, such as name, phone number and addresses, only to complete a job. Use them only for that job. Don't keep, share or use them afterwards, or contact customers for any other purpose.

5.2 Each party will handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and QareGO's [Privacy Policy](https://qarego.com/privacy).

## 6. Earnings and commission

6.1 **Rides and parcels.** You earn the fare shown, minus QareGO's commission. The commission is **[15]%** of the fare unless QareGO tells you otherwise.

6.2 **Store deliveries.** You earn the delivery fee shown. QareGO takes **[no]** commission from the delivery fee unless QareGO tells you otherwise.

6.3 **Shared jobs.** When a laundry or similar job is done by two riders (one collects, one returns), the delivery pay is split equally between them.

6.4 **Changes.** QareGO may change commission rates or how fares are calculated by giving you at least **[14] days'** notice in the app or by SMS. The offer always shows what you will earn for that job before you accept.

## 7. Payments and your wallet

7.1 **Cash jobs.** You collect the amount the app shows. For store orders, you pay the store its share when you collect the order. QareGO's commission (and, for store orders, QareGO's share of the order) is recorded as owed by you in your QareGO wallet.

7.2 **Mobile money store deliveries.** Your delivery pay is sent to the mobile money number on your account when you complete the delivery. If the transfer fails, the amount is added to your QareGO wallet instead.

7.3 **Mobile money rides and parcels.** When the customer pays, the fare minus commission is added to your QareGO wallet.

7.4 **Cash out.** You may cash out your positive wallet balance to your mobile money number at any time, subject to the minimum shown in the app (currently GH₵1).

7.5 **What you owe.** You must pay what you owe QareGO promptly, by top-up in the app or as QareGO directs. If you owe more than the limit shown in the app (currently GH₵100), you can't go on duty until you pay. QareGO may set off what you owe against money it holds for you.

7.6 **Paid in cash instead.** If a customer who chose mobile money pays you in cash, mark it in the app ("Customer paid me cash"). Only do so if you actually received the cash. A false claim is a serious breach of this agreement.

7.7 **Records.** The Earnings screen shows every job, commission, payment and wallet movement. Tell QareGO within **[30] days** if you think something is wrong.

## 8. Cancellations

8.1 If a store cancels an order after you've accepted the delivery, QareGO credits you for the trip: half the delivery fee once you've accepted, and the full delivery fee once you've reached the store or are carrying the order.

8.2 If you can't complete a job you've accepted, tell the customer and QareGO support straight away. Repeatedly cancelling or abandoning jobs may lead to suspension.

## 9. Loss, damage and accidents

9.1 You are responsible for loss of or damage to an order or parcel while it is in your care, unless it was caused by something outside your control. QareGO may recover the cost of a lost or damaged order from you after giving you a chance to respond.

9.2 You are responsible for your vehicle, your own safety and your insurance. Report any accident or safety incident during a job to QareGO support as soon as you safely can.

9.3 To the extent the law allows, QareGO is not liable for loss or injury arising from your work, your vehicle or your conduct. You agree to cover QareGO for claims arising from your breach of this agreement or the law.

## 10. Suspension and ending the agreement

10.1 You may stop using QareGO and end this agreement at any time. Amounts owed by either side remain payable.

10.2 QareGO may suspend your account, or end this agreement, if you break it or the law, if your documents are no longer valid, if you owe more than the limit, or after serious or repeated complaints. In serious cases (safety, fraud, abuse, tampering with orders) QareGO may suspend you straight away. QareGO will tell you why, and you may ask for a review.

## 11. General

11.1 **Changes.** QareGO may update this agreement by giving you at least **[14] days'** notice in the app or by SMS. If you keep going on duty after the change takes effect, you accept it.

11.2 **Notices** may be sent by SMS, WhatsApp, push notification or in the app, to the contact details on your account.

11.3 **Disputes.** The parties will first try to settle any dispute in good faith. If they can't within **[30] days**, `[mediation / arbitration under the Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798) / the courts of Ghana]`.

11.4 **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

11.5 This agreement, with the Terms of Service, is the whole agreement between the parties about its subject.
