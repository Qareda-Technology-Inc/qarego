# Why use QareGO

**One app for getting around and getting things delivered in Ghana.** Book a ride, send a parcel, or order food, groceries, medicine, cooking gas and laundry, all from QareGO.

## Everything in one app

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏍️ **Rides** | Motorbike, pragya or car, to wherever you're going |
| 📦 **Parcels** | Send a parcel, or have one picked up and brought to you |
| 🍔 **Food** | Order from restaurants and kitchens near you |
| 🛒 **Groceries** | Shop from local stores and get it delivered |
| 💊 **Pharmacy** | Order from licensed pharmacies, with your prescription uploaded in the app |
| 🔥 **Cooking gas** | Get your cylinder refilled, or exchanged for a full one |
| 👕 **Laundry** | A rider collects your clothes and brings them back clean |

## Why people choose QareGO

**You see the price first**
The app shows the fare, or the order total with every fee, **before you confirm**. That's what you pay.

**Pay the way you like**
Pay in **cash** when your trip ends or your order arrives, or with **mobile money** or **card** in the app.

**Your code keeps you safe**
You get a **4-digit code** for each trip or delivery. Share it only with the rider in front of you. Your ride or order is only confirmed with the right code, so it reaches the right person.

**Follow every step**
Watch your rider on the map, and call or chat with them in the app. Store orders show each step: accepted, being prepared, on the way.

**Order now, later, or pick it up**
Schedule an order for a time that suits you, or collect it yourself from the store.

**Fair refunds**
If a store order you paid for by mobile money or card is cancelled, the money goes back to you automatically.

## Getting started

1. Download **QareGO** from Google Play or the App Store.
2. Sign in with your phone number and the code we text you.
3. Choose what you need, and you're done.
