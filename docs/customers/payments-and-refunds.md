# Payments, cancellations and refunds

A plain-language summary for customers. The full rules are in the [Terms of Service](https://qarego.com/terms).

## The price you see is the price you pay

Before you confirm, the app shows:
- **Rides and parcels:** the fare for your chosen vehicle.
- **Store orders:** the items, a service fee and the delivery fee (no delivery fee for pickup).

That's what you pay, unless you change the booking.

| Fee | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Fare** | Rides and parcels. Based on the vehicle and distance. |
| **Delivery fee** | Store orders. Based on distance. Paid to the rider. |
| **Service fee** | Store orders. Currently 8% of the items, at least GH₵2 and at most GH₵12. |

## How you can pay

| Method | When you pay |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | To the rider when your trip ends or your order arrives. |
| **Mobile money or card** | **Store orders:** when you place the order. **Rides and parcels:** after the trip, in the app. |

Mobile money and card payments are processed by **Paystack**. QareGO never sees your mobile money PIN or card number.

**Unpaid trips.** If you chose mobile money for a ride or parcel and haven't paid, you can't book or order again until you pay. The app shows a **Pay now** reminder. If you paid the rider in cash instead, the rider records it and the reminder goes away.

## Cancelling

| What | When you can cancel |
|---|---|
| **Ride or parcel** | While we're finding a rider, or from the trip screen. |
| **Store order** | Until the store accepts it. After that, contact the store or QareGO support. |

A store may also cancel an order it can't fulfil, for example when an item has run out.

A store order you chose to pay by mobile money or card is cancelled automatically if the payment isn't finished within about 15 minutes. You aren't charged; if a payment does go through after that, it's refunded automatically.

Repeated cancellations or no-shows may limit your use of QareGO.

## Refunds

- **Store order cancelled (by you, the store or QareGO) after you paid by mobile money or card:** refunded **automatically** to the same mobile money number or card. It can take a few days to show, depending on your network or bank.
- **Paid a trip twice** (you paid the rider in cash, then also paid in the app): the app payment is refunded automatically.
- **Problem with an order** (missing items, wrong order, damaged): contact support as soon as you can, with your order number and photos if possible. We'll look into it with the store.
