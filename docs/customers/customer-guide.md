## 1. Getting started

1. **Download QareGO** from Google Play or the App Store.
2. **Sign in** with your phone number. We text you a one-time code; enter it to sign in. There's no password to remember.
3. **Add your name** so riders and stores know who you are.
4. **Allow location** while using the app, so we can suggest your pickup point and show stores and riders near you.

You must be at least 18 to use QareGO.

## 2. Booking a ride

1. Tap **Where to?** and choose your destination. Check your pickup point.
2. Choose your vehicle: **motorbike**, **pragya** or **car**. Each shows its price.
3. Choose **cash** or **mobile money**, and confirm.
4. We find a rider near you. You'll see their name, photo, vehicle and where they are.
5. When the rider arrives, **give them your 4-digit code**. Only then does the trip start.
6. At the end, pay the fare: cash to the rider, or mobile money in the app.

You can cancel while we're finding a rider, or from the trip screen. Please cancel before the rider reaches you: cancelling after they've arrived counts toward a limit, and too many late cancels pause ride bookings for a few days.

## 3. Sending or receiving a parcel

1. Choose **Parcel**, then **Send** (a rider takes your parcel somewhere) or **Receive** (a rider collects something and brings it to you). The Parcel screen also shows any parcel on its way and lets you **send again** to someone you've sent to before.
2. Enter the pickup and drop-off, the other person's name and phone number, and what the parcel is. You can add a note and a photo. Instead of typing an address, you can tap one of the **parcel points** listed under the address boxes (bus stations, malls and shops QareGO has added), nearest first.
3. Pick the **size** (document, small, medium or large). Large parcels go by tricycle, the rest by motorbike, and each size shows its price. You can also say what's inside and mark it **fragile**.
4. Confirm how you'll pay. When sending, you can let QareGO **text the receiver** the delivery code and the rider's name once a rider takes the parcel, or share the code yourself from the tracking screen.
5. The person receiving the parcel gives the rider the **delivery code** to confirm it arrived.

Don't send anything illegal or dangerous: weapons, drugs, explosives, flammable items, stolen goods, live animals, or cash and valuables. Riders may refuse a parcel that breaks these rules.

## 4. Ordering from a store

1. Choose **Food**, **Groceries** or **Pharmacy**, and pick a store near you.
2. Add items to your cart. Choose sizes and extras where offered.
3. In your cart, choose:
   - **Delivery** to your address, **pickup** from the store (if the store offers it), or a **scheduled** time;
   - **cash** or **mobile money**.
4. Check the total and place your order. It shows:
   - the **items**;
   - a **service fee** (8% of the items, at least GH₵2 and at most GH₵12, at the time of writing);
   - the **delivery fee**, based on distance.
5. Follow your order: accepted, being prepared, picked up, on the way.
6. When it arrives, **give the rider your 4-digit code**.

**Mobile money orders** are paid when you place them. The store starts once your payment is confirmed. If the payment isn't finished within about 15 minutes, the order is cancelled automatically and you aren't charged.

**Pharmacy:** there are two ways to order.
- **Send a prescription.** Tap **Have a prescription?**, take a photo of it (up to 3 pages), or just write what you need. Choose a pharmacy (the nearest open one is picked for you), delivery or pickup, and cash or MoMo. Sending is free. The pharmacist replies with a price: each medicine, anything they don't have, and the total with fees. **Approve** it and the pharmacy starts packing. For MoMo you pay right after approving. **Decline** and the order is cancelled; you've paid nothing.
- **Shop the shelves.** Add products to your cart as usual. For prescription-only medicines, attach a photo of your prescription at checkout. The pharmacy checks it and decides what it can supply.

Either way, your rider doesn't see the names of your medicines.

**Cancelling:** you can cancel a store order until the store accepts it. After that, contact the store or QareGO support.

## 5. Refill gas

1. Choose **Refill gas**, then **Refill** (we collect your empty cylinder, fill it and bring it back) or **Exchange** (we bring a full cylinder and take your empty one).
2. Choose the cylinder size. For a refill, choose **Full refill** (choose how many cylinders) or **Buy an amount** (type how much gas you want, e.g. GH₵50, for one cylinder). The smallest amount is GH₵20, and the most is the price of a full refill for that size.
3. The price is shown before you confirm: the gas, one flat delivery fee and the service fee. QareGO accepts your request straight away and sends a courier.
4. **Refill:** pay the courier the gas price in cash when they collect your empty cylinder. They fill it at the nearest gas filling station and bring it back; then pay the delivery and service fee and give them your code.
5. **Exchange:** the courier brings a full cylinder from QareGO. Pay in cash on delivery, give them your code and hand over your empty cylinder.
6. You can cancel until a courier takes the job.

## 6. Laundry

1. Choose **Laundry**, then **Book a pickup** (or tap a service: wash and fold, wash and iron, dry clean, iron only). The nearest laundry is picked for you; tap **Change** to choose another.
2. Add a rough count of what's in the bag to get an **estimate**. A guess is fine.
3. **Pick a collection time.** If the laundry offers **Express**, you can pay its extra fee to get your clothes back sooner. The app shows when it'll be back.
4. A rider collects your bag. The laundry **counts your items** and may add photos and a note (for example a stain they found). If the count costs more than the estimate, you **approve the new price** before they wash. If you decline, your bag comes back unwashed and you pay the delivery only.
5. When it's clean, a rider brings it back. Your order shows each step and the **Back by** time. **Book again** on the Laundry screen repeats your last booking.

Laundry is paid in **cash** for now.

## 7. Paying

- **Cash:** pay the rider when your trip ends or your order arrives.
- **Mobile money or card:** pay securely in the app through our payment provider, Paystack. We never see your mobile money PIN or your card number.
- **Rides and parcels paid by mobile money** are paid **after** the trip. If you don't pay, you can't book or order again until you do; the app reminds you with a **Pay now** button.
- **Refunds:** if a store order you paid for by mobile money or card is cancelled, it's refunded automatically. See [payments, cancellations and refunds](payments-and-refunds.md).

## 8. Your code and your safety

- Every trip and delivery has a **4-digit code**. Give it **only to the rider in front of you**, when they pick you up or hand over your order.
- **Never share your code by phone or message.** QareGO staff will never ask for it.
- Check the rider's name, photo and vehicle before you get on.
- Use **Safety** in the app menu if you ever feel unsafe.
- Rate your trip or order afterwards. It helps us keep QareGO safe and good.

## 9. Your account

- Edit your name, email and photo under **Account**.
- Your past store orders are under **Orders**.
- Offers and news from QareGO are under the **bell** at the top of the home screen. A red dot shows how many are new. Tap an offer to open that store or dish and order it.
- You can **delete your account** any time: **Account → Delete my account**.
- How we use your information: [Privacy Policy](https://qarego.com/privacy). The rules for using QareGO: [Terms of Service](https://qarego.com/terms).
