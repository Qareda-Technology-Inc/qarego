## How to use them

- **"Why …" pages** are one-page pitches. Send them to a store or rider you're signing up, or print them.
- **Guides** explain step by step how to join and how everything works day to day, including the money. They answer most support questions.
- **Agreements** are drafts for the signed partner agreement with each store and rider. Customers agree to the public [Terms of Service](https://qarego.com/terms) when they sign up, so there is no separate customer agreement.

## Before you share them

1. **The agreements are drafts.** Have a lawyer in Ghana review them before anyone signs. Fill in everything in `[square brackets]` (company registration number, address, signatory and so on).
2. **Numbers can change.** Commission rates, fees, the debt limit and the free period for new stores are set by QareGO admin in **Settings**. The figures in these documents are the defaults at the time of writing. Each document says where the current figure is shown, and the app or portal always shows the figure that actually applies. Update these documents when you change a setting.
3. **Keep them in line with the website.** The public [Terms of Service](https://qarego.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://qarego.com/privacy) (in `website/public/`) are what customers agree to. If you change a rule in one place, change it in the other.
