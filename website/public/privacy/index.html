Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · QareGO is operated by Qaretech, Ghana

QareGO lets you book rides, send parcels, order food, groceries, pharmacy items and cooking gas, and lets riders earn by delivering them. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the QareGO app and website, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to customers, riders and store staff.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your phone number (used to sign in), name, and optionally your email address and profile photo.
  • Location: pickup and drop-off addresses you choose, and your device location while you use the app to find services near you. For riders, your live location is shared while you are on duty or on a trip so customers can follow their delivery. We do not collect location in the background when you are not using the app.
  • Trips and orders: what you booked or ordered, from which store, prices, delivery codes, status updates, cancellations and refunds.
  • Payments: the payment method you choose (cash or mobile money) and amounts. We do not store card numbers or mobile money PINs; mobile money is processed by our payment provider.
  • Photos you upload: profile photos, prescription photos for pharmacy orders, and parcel photos.
  • Rider information: vehicle details, plate number and the documents needed to approve you as a rider (for example your driver's licence), plus earnings, commission and ratings.
  • Messages and ratings: in-trip chat messages and the ratings and comments you give or receive.
  • Device information: a notification token so we can send you trip and order updates, and basic technical information needed to keep the app working.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in with a one-time SMS code.
  • To match you with riders and stores, calculate prices, show live tracking and complete deliveries.
  • To handle payments, commission, payouts, cancellations and refunds.
  • To send you updates about your trips and orders, and service announcements.
  • To keep the service safe: preventing fraud, resolving disputes, and approving or suspending rider and store accounts.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The other people in your trip or order: riders see the customer's name, phone number and pickup/drop-off locations; customers see their rider's name, vehicle and live location; stores see the customer's name, phone number and order.
  • Service providers who help us run QareGO, only for that purpose: cloud hosting and databases, maps and directions (Google Maps), push notifications (Google Firebase), SMS delivery for sign-in codes, image storage, and our mobile money payment provider.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your name, phone number, email address, profile photo, rider documents and notification tokens, and you can no longer sign in. We keep records of past trips, orders and payments without your personal details, because we need them for accounting, tax and dispute resolution.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your name, email and photo in the app under Account → Personal info.
  • Turn location access and notifications on or off in your phone's settings (some features need location to work).
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it.
  • Delete your account at any time — see how to delete your account.

We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and access is limited to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and sign-in codes private.

Children

QareGO is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and let you know in the app.
